## 4. Filtering Logic: Identifying "Alpha"
*   **Signal Over Noise:** Prioritize content that identifies **"Alpha" signals**—niche triggers like **board meeting transcripts**, price drops in specific industries, **technographic data changes**, or **regulatory/law changes** [43-46].
*   **Technical Depth:** Prefer content that includes **system architecture breakdowns**, **Miro diagrams**, or specific **API/JSON body** implementations over generic marketing fluff [47-50].
*   **Exclude "AI Slop":** Filter out generic "top 10 AI tools" lists or content promoting "Black Box" AI replacements for human strategy. Look for the **"Expert in the Loop"** philosophy [51-54].
*   **Effectiveness vs. Flash:** Favor practitioners who emphasize that "clients don't pay for crazy, they pay for effective" and focus on **ROI, speed, and precision** [55-57].
